# Spectre Roster Balance Report

Generated by `cmd/balancesim` — headless, bot-only matches over the real
(server-authoritative, deterministic) sim engine.

**Run:** `go run ./cmd/balancesim -duelseeds 60 -ffaseeds 250 -ctfseeds 200 -maxsec 120 -matrix`
**Sample:** 6,300 duels + 250 FFA + 400 CTF matches, bot difficulty = HARD.

> This is **diagnostics only**. No balance values were changed to produce it.

---

## How much to trust each signal

- **High trust** — the duel and FFA rankings *agree with each other*, and the
  extremes are stable. Where two independent measurements concur, it's real.
- **Lower trust** (read as "bots can't extract X," not "X is weak"):
  - **CTF healer A/B** — bots position/peel worse than humans, and the baseline
    (4× TANK) is the strongest possible fighter wall. This is the bot-AI confound.
  - **Aim-dependent ranged chars** (SCORPION, SERPENT) — bots fire with wobble; a
    human sniper would rate higher.
  - **Flight chars** (FALCON) — bots underuse altitude/kiting.
- **Misleading column** — `ACC` is meaningless for melee characters
  (T-REX/MINOTAUR/TURTLE/GORILLA/ELEPHANT read ~0–30% because melee "shots" don't
  register as hits). Only compare accuracy among ranged characters.

## The structural finding

TANK is the stated "balanced, true-to-scale" baseline — and it beats most of the
roster **90–100%** head-to-head (rank 3, 83% overall). MANTIS and HUMANOID sit
*above* even that.

So this isn't "2 characters are OP." It's that **the three generalist bipeds
(TANK-chassis) are the yardstick, and ~10 of the specialty creatures are tuned well
below it.** The fix space is mostly "lift the floor," not "nerf the top."

## Tiers (duels, 1v1 — cleanest isolation)

| Tier | Characters (win%) |
|---|---|
| **Above baseline** | MANTIS 91 · HUMANOID 88 |
| **Baseline / strong** | TANK 83 · INSECT 68 · MINOTAUR 64 · TURTLE 54 |
| **Below** | GORILLA 46 · T-REX 42 · CRAB 38 · ELEPHANT 32 · FALCON 32 · SCORPION 31 |
| **Underpowered** | SERPENT 17 · TIGER 15 · **OCTOPOD 0.2** |

The top 3 are a tight rock-paper-scissors among themselves (MANTIS beats HUMANOID
52%, loses to TANK 38%) — healthy. **OCTOPOD loses to everyone** (a single
non-zero cell in its matrix row).

## FFA corroboration + cross-mode movers

Only 6 characters ever take the top-fragger slot:
HUMANOID 26% · MANTIS 24% · INSECT 20% · TANK 15% · T-REX 10% · GORILLA 5%;
everyone else ≈ 0.

- **T-REX and GORILLA climb in FFA** vs duels — melee bruisers cleave better in a
  crowd than 1v1.
- **MINOTAUR / TURTLE** are good in duels (tanky) but take 0% of FFA top-frags —
  defensive kits don't rack kills in a brawl. Probably working as designed.

## Healers (low-confidence, bot-driven)

| Healer | team0 kill-win% | avg kill-diff | heal/game |
|---|---|---|---|
| BUTTERFLY | 45% | −3.8 | 404 |
| STAG | 16% | −8.2 | 247 |

Under *bot* control, neither healer fully pays for the 4th gun it replaces; STAG
lags butterfly badly (short-range aura heals less). **But** human teams extract far
more from healers than bots do — treat this as "bots don't use them well, and STAG
< BUTTERFLY," not a verdict that healers are underpowered.

## Things worth a closer look (flags, not fixes)

- **OCTOPOD** — lowest damage on the roster (497/game) and a slow-ink kit. Like the
  healers, it may be a *team-control* character any solo/FFA test rates near zero.
  Decide: should it be self-sufficient, or accepted as a utility pick?
- **TIGER** — has lifesteal yet sits at 15%; bleed/heal looks undertuned.
- **STAG vs BUTTERFLY** — even granting the bot confound, stag is strictly worse
  than the other healer.
