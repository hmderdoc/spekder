# Spectre Roster Balance Report

Generated by `cmd/balancesim` — headless, bot-only matches over the real
(server-authoritative, deterministic) sim engine.

**Run:** `go run ./cmd/balancesim -duelseeds 60 -ffaseeds 250 -ctfseeds 200 -maxsec 120 -matrix`
**Sample:** 6,300 duels + 250 FFA + 400 CTF matches, bot difficulty = HARD.
**Code:** per-character stats (chassis retired) · thematic per-character secondaries · tank-tank separation. (HEAD `e9a9959`.)

> Diagnostics only — no balance values were changed to produce this. The buff/nerf calls are yours.

## How much to trust each signal

- **High trust:** the duel and FFA rankings agree on the extremes, and they're stable across seeds.
- **Lower trust** (read as "bots can't extract X," not "X is weak"):
  - **CTF healer A/B** — bots peel/position worse than humans, and the baseline (4× TANK) is the strongest possible wall. The bot-AI confound.
  - **Aim-dependent ranged** (SCORPION, SERPENT) — bots fire with wobble; a human would rate them higher.
  - **Combo/mobility kits** (TIGER's POUNCE dash + bleed; flight) — bots underuse them.
- **`ACC` is meaningless for melee** (T-REX/MINOTAUR/TURTLE/GORILLA/TIGER read ~0–20% because melee "shots" don't register as hits).

## Where the roster stands

The refactor + secondaries **lifted the floor dramatically** vs. the original chassis-era baseline (OCTOPOD went 0.2% → 37%; the old cannon-only top-4 monopoly broke up). What remains is a narrower problem at the two ends:

- **Ceiling:** the generalists — **HUMANOID 89%, TANK 84%, MINOTAUR 83%** in duels. HUMANOID (GUN) and TANK (nerfed CANNON) still out-stat the field; MINOTAUR's barrier + hammer make it nearly unkillable 1v1.
- **Floor:** **CRAB 2%, TIGER 16%, SERPENT 17%.**

**CRAB is the standout problem (1.9%).** Its hybrid identity isn't translating: SAND is pure utility (0 damage), so its whole offense is the 2-charge CLAW melee, and the bot can't land it often enough to win — it loses to nearly everyone. It needs real help: SAND chip, more CLAW damage/charges, or better closing.

## FFA (top-fragger share; even baseline 6.7%)

Only the raw damage-dealers take the slot: **T-REX 26 · GORILLA 26 · HUMANOID 25 · TANK 17**; everyone else ≈ 0–2%. Melee bruisers (T-REX, GORILLA) cleave far better in a crowd than 1v1 — the inverse of the defensive picks (TURTLE/MINOTAUR/ELEPHANT) that do nothing for top-frag in FFA.

Under *bot* control, trading a 4th gun for a healer loses the kill trade; STAG lags butterfly badly. Treat as "bots don't use healers well + STAG < BUTTERFLY," not a verdict that healers are underpowered — humans extract far more.

## Tuning targets (flags, not fixes)

1. **CRAB** — broken (2%). Give SAND chip damage and/or buff the CLAW (damage/charge/regen); verify the bot actually closes to claw range.
2. **TIGER / SERPENT** (~16%) — TIGER's DPS/POUNCE kit and SERPENT's venom under-deliver; both want numeric love (SCRATCH/bleed up for tiger, VENOM up for serpent).
3. **HUMANOID / TANK** (~84–89%) — the generalists out-stat the field; trim GUN/CANNON output or their chassis stats.
4. **MINOTAUR** (83% duels) — barrier + hammer too safe 1v1; consider barrier HP / cooldown.
